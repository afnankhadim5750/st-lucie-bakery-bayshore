Our story

A family bakery, thirty-five years on Bayshore.

St Lucie Bakery opened its first shop in 1989, in the Morningside Shoppes plaza, when Kelley Arciprete and her husband set out to bake the Italian-American food they grew up with. Her husband’s family has a long history in baking, and that history is still in the ovens today.

The first shop on Port St. Lucie Boulevard closed in 2022. The Bayshore Boulevard bakery is the one to visit now — the same recipes, the same counter, and the same regulars, some of whom were brought here as children by their grandparents and now bring their own children.

  • Everything baked in the shop, fresh through the day
  • An Italian market beside the bakery case
  • A deli counter with cold cuts, subs and ready meals

Saturday mornings

Giant cinnamon rolls, and the crumb cake regulars swear by.

Saturday is cinnamon-roll morning at Bayshore. The rolls come out giant, warm and iced, in the original and in specialty flavours — strawberry cream cheese, apple with candied walnuts, pistachio cream cheese and blueberry among them.

And if you ask the regulars what to take home, most of them will say the same thing: the crumb cake. One reviewer put it simply — the best crumb cake they have had.
